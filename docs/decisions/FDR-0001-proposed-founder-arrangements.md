## 3. Technical-founder commercial direction

The technical founder subsequently confirmed the following direction on 2026-11-21. This is evidence of that founder's position only. It does not record DePrince's independent approval, amend the unresolved founder agreement, authorize spending or implementation, or establish authority to bind a future entity.

1. Veyra begins with **limited free private testing**. No subscription sale, checkout, live payment, paid entitlement, customer, or revenue claim is part of that phase.
2. The intended public commercial model is **freemium**: a genuinely useful free lesson offering plus genuine paid subscription features whose boundaries must be approved and implemented truthfully.
3. The commercial objective is sustainable, verifiable recurring revenue and a possible future sale through TrustMRR, subject to Veyra's actual eligibility and the platform's then-current requirements. This is an objective, not evidence of eligibility, valuation, sale readiness, subscribers, retention, revenue, or MRR.
4. T2.1 is a non-billing technical bootstrap. It does not authorize a payment SDK, checkout, webhook, subscription, paid entitlement, revenue-accounting integration, or live payment test.
5. A paid public launch requires real subscription billing, lawful payment operations, verified provider and market eligibility, approved pricing/terms, and verifiable revenue accounting. A free private test cannot satisfy those paid-launch gates.
6. The eight-track roadmap and intended international ages-13+ audience remain unchanged.
7. No adult or teenager may be recruited, contacted, interviewed, observed for research, or asked for research data until the appropriate research safeguards for that activity and cohort are approved. Teen-specific safeguards remain additional requirements rather than an assumption that adult safeguards are sufficient.
8. Revenue, subscribers, customers, retention, reviews, MRR, provider approval, and marketplace eligibility must never be fabricated or inferred from plans, private testers, screenshots, sandbox events, or repository activity.

The earlier table remains a record of the original discussion inputs. This section clarifies the technical founder's current commercial position but does not convert any topic into a mutually approved founder agreement.
