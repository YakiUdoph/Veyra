## 3. Unresolved founder agreement subjects

The following require explicit mutual agreement and qualified review where appropriate:

1. IP assignment and ownership structure, including existing and future code, content, brand, designs, domains, data, and contractor contributions.
2. Founder responsibilities, time commitments, deliverables, review obligations, contribution expectations, and how changes are approved.
3. Expense categories, contribution ratios, per-founder and aggregate limits, pre-approval thresholds, evidence, reimbursement timing, tax treatment, and responsibility for unauthorized spending.
4. Revenue distribution, founder compensation, salaries/fees, expense priority, retained earnings, taxes, and when distributions may occur.
5. Deadlock resolution, escalation, mediation/arbitration or other agreed process, and handling of urgent operational or security decisions.
6. Founder departure, incapacity, non-performance, vesting or repurchase if applicable, valuation, ownership transfer restrictions, right of first refusal, and treatment of assigned IP/access.
7. Ownership, custody, recovery, least-privilege access, and transfer procedures for accounts, domain/DNS, repositories, email, banking/payment, hosting, database, storage, analytics, secrets, backups, and other infrastructure.
8. Business entity, incorporation/registration jurisdiction, authority to bind it, banking, accounting/tax responsibilities, contracts, and applicable legal review.
9. Paid-launch approval authority and safeguards for users aged 13–17, including country-specific eligibility/consent, who may contract/pay, guardian involvement if legally required, pricing/refunds/cancellation, marketing, provider eligibility, and evidence needed before subscriptions are enabled.
